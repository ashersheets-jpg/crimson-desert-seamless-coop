**Update: shared world playtest 0.1.0-alpha+09a188c3 (fighting far from the host, lying down when downed, no more packet files)**
Please use this build instead of every earlier one. You and your co-op partner both need it. It keeps everything from 3c46bec5.

Fixed:
- **A guest far from the host could not fight**: more than 80 m from the host it took no damage and its hits did nothing. Now both count.
- **The mod wrote big files into the game folder**: playing solo (or sitting at the title) with the mod installed recorded network packets to `bin64\cdmp_packets_....log` / `.bin` with no size limit. It no longer does. You can delete those files.
- The line at the bottom of the title menu was cut off.

New:
- **A downed player lies on the ground** (they used to stand upright at 0 health). Your friend sees it too, and can hold Interact next to you to get you up; Q and U work as before.

- `CrimsonDesert-SeamlessCoop-0.1.0-alpha+09a188c3-shared-world.zip`: this is the one you want. Run its installer with the game closed.
- `...-shared-world-mod-manager.zip`: only for manual / Vortex / MO2 installs.

Tested with two copies of the game on one PC (NVIDIA card): first launch, a real install, XeSS and FSR frame generation, a new save, chests, enemy loot, going down and getting up, a fight 108 m from the host. Not tested here: two PCs over the internet, AMD and Intel cards, long sessions. Please tell us in #bug-reports how it goes (and send the report zip if anything goes wrong).
