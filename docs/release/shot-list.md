# Shot list for the Nexus page (T27; rewritten 2026-11-22 for the shared world)

Take these at 1920x1080 or higher, HUD on, ideally during a two-PC session (each shot from the screen named). The name
over the friend shows the Steam display name: use names you are happy to show. Many can also come from the two-copy
test runs (build/native/shots), cropped to one game window.

| # | Shot | Where / how | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Main image:** both characters side by side on a ridge at golden hour | Any open hilltop, game time ~18:00 (the host's time) | The two players, the name over the friend |
| 2 | The Co-op menu with the party | Title screen, both in the party (crown on the host) | Joining from the title |
| 3 | Start Co-op's three choices | Host: Start Co-op | Continue / From my solo progress / New co-op save |
| 4 | Fighting the same bandits | Rodbel dock camp, both mid-swing | Shared enemies |
| 5 | The joined player at a merchant | Hernand butcher, the joined player's shop open | Shops for the friend |
| 6 | Riding together | Both mounted on a road (or the friend on Blackstar) | Mounts |
| 7 | Hold Interact on your friend | The friend in the middle of the screen, the menu open | Challenges, give / trade |
| 8 | Duel | A duel at "Fight!" | The ring, the friend as opponent |
| 9 | Down and revived | The game's death screen, then the friend holding Interact by you | Revives |
| 10 | The Co-op tab | Pause menu > Co-op | Session, settings |
| 11 | A chest share on the map | The pin of a chest the friend opened | Items for each of you |
| 12 | A door in town | The joined player walking out of the Hernand tavern | One shared world |
| 13 | Boss fight together (after B07) | The host's next story boss | Both in the arena |
| 14 | Controller prompts | A pad in hand, holding A on the friend | Pad prompts |

Optional video (60-90 s): invite from the title, Start Co-op, fight a camp, ride, a duel, a revive. No music from
the game's soundtrack in an uploaded video unless the platform allows it.

Before uploading, check each shot for: other people's names in the Steam overlay, notifications from Discord or
Windows, and the FPS counter (top left in test copies).
