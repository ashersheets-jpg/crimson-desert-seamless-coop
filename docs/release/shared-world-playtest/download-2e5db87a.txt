**Update: shared world playtest 0.1.0-alpha+2e5db87a, for game 1.0.0.3663**
Please use this one instead of 66985463; **both players need the same build** (the title menu tells you if your friend has another one).

New, **experimental and off by default: Story scenes together** (the host turns it on before starting co-op: title screen > Co-op > Co-op Settings > "Story scenes together (experimental)").
- Each of you gets your own story scenes and quest steps, and a story scene **waits until both of you are there**. When one of you reaches it, both screens say so; the friend can hold **Interact** to go to the one who waits.
- When you are both there, each of you holds **Interact** to start, and **both games play the cutscene at the same moment**.
- The one who waits can also hold **Interact** to go on alone; the friend plays that scene later.
- Tested on one PC with two copies of the game, at the prologue's cabin only (both together, the host alone, the joined player alone). Not tried yet: later story scenes, story bosses, two PCs. If you turn it on, please tell us how far it gets.

Fixed:
- The joining friend could sit at the title for minutes after the host was in: the friend's game ignored the mod's own Play press under the "waiting for the host" screen.
- The host's "your friend could not join" card stayed up when the friend got in after a slow join.
- Steam presence could switch itself off after one Steam error.

- `...2e5db87a-shared-world.zip`: the one you want. Run its installer with the game closed.
- `...-shared-world-mod-manager.zip`: only for manual / Vortex / MO2 installs.

Tested with two copies of the game on one PC (Nvidia), the full checklist (story scenes together off, as it ships). Not tested: two PCs over the internet with this build, AMD / Intel cards, a real PlayStation controller, an evening-long session.
