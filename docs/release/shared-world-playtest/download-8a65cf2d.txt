Thanks @Borque for the combat report:
- **Enemies ignored the joining friend:** confirmed. The host's game ran the enemies, and they went for the host even while only the friend fought them (our test: 27 blows on the idle host, 3 on the friend). Now an enemy the friend is fighting runs on the friend's game and fights back; when the host takes over, it goes back to the host.

With a friend in, enemies also have 1.5x health for both of you (Co-op Settings, Combat & Loot, "Enemy health with a guest"). Each of you brings your own character, so weaker gear or level means more hits. Still twice? Tell us.
- Still to look at: the boar lying 3 m apart, the mission NPC under the cart, the frozen run animation.
