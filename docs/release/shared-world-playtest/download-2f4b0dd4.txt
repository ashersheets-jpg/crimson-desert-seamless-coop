**Update: shared world playtest 0.1.0-alpha+2f4b0dd4 (the guest can fight, chests and gear for each player, give items, crash guards)**
Please use this build instead of 24661515. You and your co-op partner both need it.

Fixed:
- **Enemies ignored the guest's attacks** (thanks Borque): hostile people were protected from the guest until the host had hit that very enemy. Now only people the host has talked to or traded with are protected.
- **The guest became a criminal for helping**: its hits and kills on the host's enemies counted as "Crime: Assault" / "Crime: Murder". Now the guest is judged the way the host would be.
- **Item duplication**: an item one of you dropped could be taken by both. Now it goes to whoever picks it up.
- **Two crashes**: a guest crash right after entering the host's world, and a host crash right after looting.
- The stray RB icon in the pause menu's tab row is gone.

New:
- **Give an item to your friend**: stand next to them, open your inventory, press Discard on an item and choose "Give to <name>". The item keeps its upgrades, durability and dye.
- **Chests are for each of you**: when one of you opens a chest, the other gets the same content at the chest (at once when you stand together; if you are elsewhere you get a message and a map pin). The chest still opens for both.
- **Gear lying in the world is for each of you**: an item the game placed in the world can be taken once by each player, as herbs already could.
- **Shared stash** (Co-op tab): the host's warehouse, usable by both from anywhere once the host has unlocked it in the story. Untested with an unlocked warehouse.

- `CrimsonDesert-SeamlessCoop-0.1.0-alpha+2f4b0dd4-shared-world.zip`: this is the one you want. Run its installer with the game closed.
- `...-shared-world-mod-manager.zip`: only for manual / Vortex / MO2 installs.

Tested with two copies of the game on one PC. Please tell us in #bug-reports how it goes (and send the report zip if anything goes wrong).
