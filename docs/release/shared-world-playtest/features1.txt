**Crimson Desert Seamless Co-op+ (alpha): feature list, shared world playtest build**
Two-player co-op (Steam, game 1.0.0.3576). New in this build: you both play in **one shared world**. This is the basic part of the big update; more comes later.
**Works** = checked with two copies of the game on one PC (still report what you see)
**Needs testing** = built, needs two real players on two PCs. Please try these!
**Not yet** = not in this build

**Joining and saves**
- Co-op menu at the title screen: Invite Friends, then Start Co-op; both games restart once into the shared world: Works
- Joining over Steam between two PCs: Needs testing (never tried on two PCs)
- Radmin VPN / Direct IP from the Co-op menu: Needs testing
- A co-op save per friend; your normal saves are not touched: Works
- Continue Our Co-op Save: the host carries on from the last co-op autosave: Works
- The guest keeps their own character and progress between sessions: Works
- If the guest leaves or drops, the host keeps playing: Works
- Joining mid-game: Not yet (title screen only)

**One shared world**
- The same NPCs and townsfolk for both of you; the guest can talk to them: Works
- Merchants: the guest can buy and sell, also when the host is far away: Works
- Quests: each player keeps their own progress: Works
- Revive a downed friend: hold E next to them: Works
- The same enemies for both, fighting both of you: Needs testing
- Long sessions (an hour or more): Needs testing
